About

Hey ! I’m Victor Dalbin, also known as Vyko, a digital and traditional artist. Passionate about drawing, illustration, and 3D, I enjoy experimenting with different techniques and continuing to learn through my artistic practice.

For me, art, in all its forms, is a way to express what I feel. It’s a suspended moment where my ideas take shape and where I feel fully present.
